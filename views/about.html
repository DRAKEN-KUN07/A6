Our Story

About FreshFold Laundry

Founded in 2021 to give busy students, families, and professionals back their weekends with dependable, environmentally responsible garment care.

Quality Without Shortcuts

Every laundry order is tracked individually—never mixed with another customer's load. Our facility calibrates water hardness and drying heat by fabric type so cotton stays soft, colors stay bright, and elastics last longer.

From delicate knitwear to everyday gym clothes, our specialists follow garment care labels carefully and inspect every stack prior to packaging.

Eco-Conscious Operations

Traditional dry cleaning relies on harsh perchloroethylene solvents. FreshFold uses biodegradable plant-derived detergents, closed-loop water filtration, and high-efficiency heat recovery dryers that cut water and energy use by over 40%.

  • Phosphate-free and dye-free options
  • Reusable zippered laundry bags
  • Electric local delivery vans
